4. GNSS Accuracy, Technical Tolerances & Environmental Limitations

Satellite-derived velocity measurements are subject to inherent physical and atmospheric tolerances beyond the control of AeroSpeed. You acknowledge that readings may exhibit variances or latency due to:

  • Multipath Signal Interference: Radio wave reflections off tall skyscrapers, concrete overpasses, billboards, or rock faces in mountainous terrain.
  • Atmospheric Scintillation: Ionospheric and tropospheric delays affecting satellite signal propagation velocity.
  • Satellite Dilution of Precision (GDOP / HDOP): Geometric arrangement and quantity of satellites visible in the sky above your device receiver.
  • Physical Obstructions & Tunnels: Complete loss of GNSS signal reception when entering underground passages, enclosed tunnels, dense tree canopies, or multi-level parking garages.
  • Browser Hardware Throttling: Operating system power-saving modes or background tab prioritization limits that degrade Geolocation API refresh frequencies.
