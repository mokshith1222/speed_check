About AeroSpeed Telematics Labs

Pioneering accessible, satellite-derived vehicle velocity analytics and automotive safety science.

1. Our Founding Mission

Founded in 2021 by a team of aerospace and automotive telemetry engineers, AeroSpeed Telematics Labs was established to bridge the transparency gap between onboard vehicle instrumentation and true physical velocity. Factory vehicle speedometers are subject to intentional regulatory over-indication biases, tire wear deviations, and transmission gear changes.

Our goal was simple yet ambitious: build a zero-install, zero-cost, open-standard browser telematics utility that leverages modern multi-constellation GNSS (GPS, Galileo, GLONASS, BeiDou) receiver chips to calculate genuine road speed through Doppler frequency shifts and high-accuracy geodesic triangulation.

3. Technical Testing & Calibration Laboratory

Every formula, algorithmic filter, and educational guide published on AeroSpeed undergoes empirical validation at our testing facility located in San Jose, California. Our calibration protocols utilize:

  • Dual-Frequency RTK GNSS Receivers: Capable of 100Hz position logging with sub-centimeter spatial accuracy to benchmark browser Geolocation API latencies.
  • Chassis Dynamometers: Calibrated roller dynamometers to measure wheel slip ratios and drive-axle rotational speeds against satellite Doppler velocity readings.
  • Optical Speed Traps & Radar LiDAR Guns: Calibrated Stalker dual-antenna Ka-band Doppler radar units to simulate real-world traffic enforcement scenarios and verify speed alarm thresholds.

4. Editorial Integrity & Fact-Checking Standards

We believe automotive safety information must be held to the highest scientific standards. All technical articles in our Knowledge Center adhere to strict editorial policies:

  1. Peer-Reviewed Research Grounding: All statements regarding aerodynamics, fuel consumption curves, radar physics, and legal speedometer tolerances cite verifiable standards (such as UNECE Regulation 39, SAE J670 vehicle dynamics terminology, and NIST measurement guidelines).
  2. No Sponsored Endorsements: We do not accept paid consideration or third-party sponsorship to promote specific automotive products or radar detectors. Our reviews and technical guides remain 100% objective.
  3. Periodic Accuracy Audits: Articles are reviewed semi-annually to reflect updates in satellite constellation launches (e.g., GPS III, Galileo second generation) and evolving traffic safety regulations.

5. Commitment to User Privacy

Unlike many smartphone speedometer apps that harvest and sell location tracking databases to insurance brokers or marketing agencies, AeroSpeed operates with absolute client-side data sovereignty. Your GPS coordinates are processed exclusively in your device's browser memory and are never uploaded to our servers.
