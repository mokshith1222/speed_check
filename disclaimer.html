Automotive Safety & Telematics Accuracy Disclaimer

Important technical caveats regarding GNSS satellite speed measurement, vehicular safety laws, and driving liability.

1. Primary Speedometer vs Secondary Reference

The AeroSpeed GPS Speedometer web application is designed solely as a supplementary, secondary telematics reference and educational tool. It is NOT an officially certified primary automotive instrument and must never be relied upon as the sole metric of vehicle speed while driving on public highways or private roadways.

Always defer to your vehicle's factory instrument cluster, maintain awareness of surrounding traffic, and obey all officially posted speed limits.

2. Safe Driving and Mobile Device Operation

WARNING: Distracted driving is a leading cause of severe traffic accidents and fatalities worldwide.
  • Never adjust settings, type, or interact with your mobile screen while operating a moving vehicle.
  • Mount your mobile device securely in an approved dashboard cradle or phone holder before putting the vehicle into gear.
  • Ensure that using the Heads-Up Display (HUD) mode does not obscure your peripheral vision, create distracting reflections during daylight, or violate municipal windshield obstruction laws.

3. Technical Limitations of GPS Geolocation

Browser-based GPS calculations rely on multi-constellation satellite signals and mobile device hardware sensors. Velocity readings may be delayed or distorted under conditions including:

  • Passage through tunnels, underpasses, or underground parking garages where satellite line-of-sight is blocked.
  • Urban canyon effects caused by reflective glass skyscrapers in dense metropolitan centers.
  • Extreme atmospheric weather disturbances or solar geomagnetic activity affecting satellite radio signal transmission.
  • Low mobile battery conditions causing operating system power throttling of GNSS chip refresh rates.
