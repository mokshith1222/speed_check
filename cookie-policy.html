COOKIE GOVERNANCE · JANUARY 2026

Cookie Policy & Consent Governance

Explanatory disclosure regarding browser cookies, local storage objects, and third-party advertising identifiers.

1. What Are Cookies?

Cookies are tiny text files stored on your computer or mobile device when you load web pages. They enable websites to recall user actions and preferences (such as unit preferences, display theme, and consent choices) over a period of time, ensuring a smooth and responsive user experience.

2. Categories of Cookies Used by AeroSpeed

Type Name / Provider Specific Purpose
Strictly Necessary aerospeed_cookie_consent Remembers your cookie consent selection so the prompt banner does not repeat on every visit.
Functional Local Storage aerospeed_theme Stores your chosen UI dark/light mode preference locally in your browser memory.
Third-Party Advertising __gads, __gpi, IDE (Google / DoubleClick) Delivers relevant contextual advertising via Google AdSense, prevents ad repetition, and measures impression efficacy.

3. Google AdSense & Third-Party Cookies

Third-party vendors, including Google, utilize cookies to serve advertisements based on your prior browsing history. Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its advertising network partners to present ads based on your visit to AeroSpeed and other websites across the web.

You can opt out of personalized Google advertising at any time by configuring your Google Ads Settings or through the cross-industry Digital Advertising Alliance WebChoices Tool.

4. Managing and Disabling Cookies in Your Browser

Most web browsers allow you to control cookie settings through their preferences menu:

  • Google Chrome: Settings > Privacy and security > Third-party cookies.
  • Mozilla Firefox: Settings > Privacy & Security > Enhanced Tracking Protection.
  • Apple Safari: Preferences > Privacy > Block all cookies.
  • Microsoft Edge: Settings > Cookies and site permissions.

Please note that blocking strictly necessary functional cookies may reset your theme and measurement unit preferences upon subsequent visits.
