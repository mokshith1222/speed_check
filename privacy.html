3. Client-Side Geolocation Processing & Zero-Storage Guarantee

The primary functionality of the AeroSpeed web application is to measure physical velocity, heading, altitude, and trip distance via satellite Global Navigation Satellite System (GNSS) hardware embedded inside your computer, smartphone, tablet, or mobile operating system.

Our Zero-Storage Telematics Architecture:

  • Client-Side Only Computation: When you grant browser permission to access your device's location, the W3C Geolocation API feeds latitude, longitude, altitude, speed, and heading coordinates directly into client-side JavaScript executing in your local browser sandbox.
  • No Server Transmission: Your real-time geographical coordinates are NEVER transmitted to, processed by, or recorded on AeroSpeed web servers or any external remote databases.
  • No Tracking History: We do not construct historical travel routes, trip histories, speed profiles, or location timelines on our infrastructure. All trip metrics (such as maximum speed, average velocity, and distance elapsed) reside purely in temporary device RAM memory and are permanently wiped when you close or reload your browser tab, unless you manually choose to export a CSV spreadsheet to your local hard drive.
